Toll Mate — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Toll Mate collects, uses and protects data when you use the application.

Data collection

Toll Mate uses device location data to detect toll roads and charging zones. Location data is processed in real time and is not stored or transmitted unless otherwise stated.

Location data and background use

Location data is used solely for the purpose of detecting toll roads and charging zones and providing reminders. Background location access is used only for toll and charging-zone detection and reminders. It is never used for advertising, tracking, profiling or any other purpose. You can disable location access at any time in your device settings, although the core functionality of the application requires it. This data remains on the user's device and is not shared with third parties.

Personal data

Toll Mate does not require users to create an account and does not collect personal identifiable information such as name, email or address.

Data storage

Toll Mate does not store location history or personal data on external servers. Journey data recorded by the app is stored locally on your device, remains under your control and can be deleted from within the app.

Third party links

The application may direct users to third party websites for payment. Toll Mate does not control and is not responsible for the privacy practices of these websites.

Legal basis for processing

Location data is processed based on user consent and the legitimate interest of providing the core functionality of the application.

User consent

By enabling location services and using the application the user consents to the processing of location data for its intended purpose.

User rights

Under applicable data protection laws users have the right to access, correct or delete their personal data. As no personal data is stored on our servers, these rights are exercised through device-level controls and the in-app deletion options.

Data security

Toll Mate uses reasonable measures to ensure data processed within the application is handled securely.

Children

The application is not intended for use by children under the age of 16.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the application constitutes acceptance of any updates. The current version is always published at this address.

Contact

For any questions regarding this policy, contact hello@bhapps.co.uk.

Jurisdiction

This policy is intended to comply with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.
