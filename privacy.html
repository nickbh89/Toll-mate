Toll Mate — Privacy Policy

How Toll Mate collects, uses and protects your data.

Last updated: 6 June 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Toll Mate collects, uses and protects data when you use the application.

Data Collection

Toll Mate uses device location data to detect toll roads and charging zones. Location data is processed in real time and is not stored or transmitted unless otherwise stated.

Location Data and Background Use

Location data is used solely to detect when you are approaching or entering a toll road, tunnel or charging zone, so the app can remind you about any applicable charge. To provide this reminder while you are driving and not actively looking at your phone, Toll Mate may access your location in the background (including when the app is closed or not in use). Background location access is used only for toll and charging-zone detection and reminders. It is never used for advertising, tracking, profiling or any other purpose. You can disable location access at any time in your device settings, though the app's core detection feature will not function without it.

Personal Data

Toll Mate does not require users to create an account and does not collect personal identifiable information such as name, email or address.

Data Storage

Toll Mate does not store location history or personal data on external servers. Any journey information you choose to keep is stored locally on your own device and can be deleted by you at any time within the app.

Third Party Links

The application may direct users to third party websites for payment. Toll Mate does not control and is not responsible for the privacy practices of these websites.

Legal Basis for Processing

Location data is processed based on user consent and the legitimate interest of providing the core functionality of the application.

User Consent

By enabling location services and using the application, the user consents to the processing of location data for its intended purpose.

User Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, users have the right to access, correct or delete their personal data. As no personal data is stored on external servers, these rights are exercised primarily through device-level controls and by deleting locally stored data within the app.

Data Security

Toll Mate uses reasonable measures to ensure data processed within the application is handled securely.

Children

The application is not intended for use by children under the age of 16.

Changes to Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the application constitutes acceptance of any updates.
